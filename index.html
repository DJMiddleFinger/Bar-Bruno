Our Story

The corner of Henry & Union, lit up again.

Bar Bruno first opened on the corner of Henry and Union in 2011. Early in 2026 it changed hands. The partners behind Greenpoint Fish & Lobster Co., Peter Juusola, Orion Russell and Vinny Milburn, refreshed the room, wrote a new menu from scratch and reopened in April.

Chef Osmar Morales runs the kitchen. He was born in Oaxaca and spent eight years at Greenpoint Fish & Lobster. His cooking leans Oaxacan and puts the seafood first, with fish from the team's own market. That means tacos by the trio, enchiladas, chile rellenos, Oaxacan mole and whole fish for the table.

Families come early. The margaritas come later. When the weather cooperates there are tables out on the sidewalk, and the game is on inside and out.
